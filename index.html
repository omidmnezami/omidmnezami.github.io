Research & applied science

Large language models,
grounded in evidence.

I work on large language models, clinical natural language processing and evidence-grounded AI agents.

As a Principal Applied Scientist at Oracle, I combine model development with coordination of clinical coding-agent evaluation, error analysis and release readiness. My research spans preference alignment training, data synthesis, multimodal learning and evolutionary optimisation.

I completed my PhD in Computer Science at Macquarie University in 2019, supervised by Professor Mark Dras, followed by postdoctoral research, machine learning teaching and postgraduate project advising at Macquarie.

Research

Themes &
contributions

Large language models & data-centric NLP

CodeLLM data synthesis, preference alignment, knowledge-augmented fine-tuning, model adaptation and long-text processing.

Evidence-grounded clinical coding agents

Diagnosis and procedure extraction, source-linked justifications, temporal reasoning, rule-guided validation and human review, supported by reproducible end-to-end evaluation.

Healthcare NLP & privacy

Clinical named entity recognition, PII/PHI de-identification, medical semantic textual similarity, training-data augmentation and interdisciplinary psychopathology research.

Multimodal learning & robust perception

Controlled image captioning, facial expression analysis, speech emotion, student engagement recognition and adversarial object-detection attacks.

Evolutionary optimisation & robotics

Diversity-guided particle swarm optimisation, including DDEPSO and DGPSO, and optimisation of biped locomotion.

real-world systems

2021–present

Principal Applied Scientist

Oracle · Sydney, Australia

  • Develop and evaluate LLM-based clinical coding agents, including evidence-linked diagnosis and procedure outputs, workflow refinement and systematic error analysis.
  • Coordinate model-improvement priorities, code reviews, release-readiness follow-up and customer feedback with engineering and clinical coding specialists.
  • Develop end-to-end and component-level evaluation workflows, regression checks and comparisons of model quality, cost and latency.
  • Led LLM preference-alignment development for healthcare; initiated, developed and released a PII/PHI de-identification service supporting medical-data acquisition.
  • Contributed to custom and pre-built clinical NER and co-invented adaptive and gazetteer/perturbation-based training-data augmentation.
  • Co-invented preference-aligned form conversion, multi-head fine-tuning and long-text methods, with published applications addressing knowledge augmentation and low-resource code alignment.
  • Co-authored the NAACL 2025 CodeLLM data-synthesis study; developed machine learning software using Python, TensorFlow and PyTorch.
